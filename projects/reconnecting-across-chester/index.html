Chester Avenue is a six-lane scar of urban renewal that prioritizes commuter speed over neighborhood connectivity. The corridor divides Hough residents from Midtown’s community anchors while encouraging suburban strip development and creating dangerous conditions for pedestrians and cyclists.

Despite AADT volumes declining by 8,000 since 2016, Chester remains unsafe. Over the same period, the first half-mile alone has seen over 100 crashes, with 31 causing injury and 1 being fatal. Many parcels along the corridor are either vacant or contain poorly maintained buildings, but public and non-profit land ownership provides opportunities for new development.

This project proposes a complete road diet with protected bike lanes, pedestrian refuge islands, and expanded green buffers. Three strategic development sites totaling 15 acres introduce affordable housing, street-activating retail, and community spaces that transform Chester from a dividing edge into a neighborhood connector.

Site Plans

Site 1: Wilson Living Center

  • Centralize community spaces and rehab youth baseball field
  • Provide at least 30% affordable townhomes and MF apts
  • Connect 55th to 59th for connectivity and additional parking

Site 2: Mixed Use Hub at 55th

  • Relocate U-Haul for mixed commercial on Chester & 55th
  • Retain light industrial uses while providing townhomes on 57th
  • Use blank facades for local art, particularly the back of Kram Kram

Site 3: Dunham Green Corridor

  • Honor Hough’s history through a new Black Avenue Center
  • Compliment the Dunham plan with mom-and-pop shops
  • Provide affordable housing through modular prefab homes
